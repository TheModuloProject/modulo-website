Privacy policy

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Who we are

This policy describes how TheModuloProject handles information associated with this website and enquiries sent to the studio. For privacy questions, contact hello@themoduloproject.com.

The project enquiry form

The form asks for your name, company, email, project description, project type, approximate budget and timeline. Name, email, project description and project type are required to prepare a useful enquiry. Other fields are optional.

The form creates an email draft in your browser. It does not submit your information to a server, store the form in local storage, or send it automatically. You choose whether to open your email app and send it, or copy the draft. Your email provider’s own privacy terms apply when you use its service.

If you contact us

When you send an enquiry by email, we receive the information you choose to share. We use it to respond, discuss your project, prepare a proposal and, where agreed, manage our work with you. Please avoid including passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry.

We retain correspondence for as long as it is needed for the enquiry or project, and for any applicable record-keeping obligations. We do not sell enquiry information. Email, hosting and other service providers may process information to provide their services; their processing locations depend on the providers used.

Your theme preference

If you choose light or dark mode, the site stores that choice in your browser using a local storage entry called modulo-theme. It contains only the selected theme. You can remove it by clearing this website’s storage. Without a saved choice, the site follows your device’s appearance preference.

Website traffic

This version of the website includes no analytics, advertising trackers, embedded third-party media or tracking cookies. Fonts and visual assets are served with the site. The hosting provider may keep standard access and security logs, such as IP addresses, request times and requested URLs, to operate and protect the service.

Your requests

You can ask us about personal information you have provided, request a correction, or ask for its deletion by emailing hello@themoduloproject.com. We will consider your request in accordance with applicable data protection requirements, including any retention obligations. We may need to verify your identity before sharing or changing information.

Changes to this policy

If the website’s functionality or our handling of information changes, we will update this page and its revision date.
